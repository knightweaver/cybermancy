    Healer's Folly¶

    A lush mountain shrub crowned by an enormous spectacular flower. Its nectar is restorative, but four powerful vines defend the bloom.

    FAST PLAYOpening: When a creature enters Very Close, raise the four Guardian Vines and become hostile while keeping the flower visibly intact.

    Default: Vine Lash intruders; use Entangling Lash when Restraining someone helps protect the flower.

    As Vines Fall: Track active vines. At three subdued, Fewer Vines, Fewer Threats reduces attack damage.

    Protect the Reward: Telegraph Do Not Cut the Vines: serious damage spoils nectar; non-injurious restraint preserves it.

    Resolution: At four subdued vines, stop attacking and allow one dose of Mercy Nectar to be harvested.

    Goal: Make the encounter a visible nonlethal puzzle where restraint and teamwork preserve a useful healing reward.

    Features¶

    Four Guardian Vines¶

    The Healer's Folly begins with 4 active vines. When a creature first moves within Very Close range, the vines rise and the plant becomes hostile. A creature within Very Close range can use an action to attempt to wrestle one vine into submission with a Strength Roll (12). On a success, subdue one active vine. On a success with Hope, the creature may instead subdue two vines. On a failure, no vine is subdued. When all four vines are subdued, the Healer's Folly stops attacking and its nectar can be safely harvested.

    Entangling Lash¶

    When the Healer's Folly makes a successful Vine Lash attack, you can mark a Stress to Restrain the target. A Restrained creature can free themselves or be freed by an ally with a successful Strength Roll (12).

    Fewer Vines, Fewer Threats¶

    The Healer's Folly can only attack creatures with its remaining active vines. Once three or more vines have been subdued, its attacks deal 1d6+1 physical damage instead.

    Do Not Cut the Vines¶

    The healing nectar is biologically linked to the four defensive vines. If the Healer's Folly marks any HP from an attack that cuts, burns, shoots, or otherwise seriously damages a vine, the nectar immediately becomes unstable and cannot be harvested as a healing salve. Non-injurious restraint, pinning, shielding, netting, or similar creative subdual does not spoil the nectar.

    Mercy Nectar¶

    If all four vines are subdued without ruining the flower, a creature can carefully harvest one dose of Mercy Nectar. Applying the nectar to a creature as an action allows them to clear 1 HP and 1 Stress. The dose remains usable after harvesting.

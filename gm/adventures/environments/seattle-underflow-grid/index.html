    Seattle Underflow Grid¶

    A vast, contaminated sewer and stormwater undercity beneath Seattle, warped by corporate dumping and lingering Resonance anomalies.

    Attribute Value
    Tier1
    TypeExploration
    Difficulty13

    Impulses¶

    • Isolate intruders
    • Pollute and mutate
    • Hide crimes and secrets
    • Rearrange safe routes
    • Surface danger into the city above

    Features¶

    Toxic Runoff Channels¶

    Pools and runoff trenches contain corrosive biotech waste. When a PC falls prone in contaminated water or remains submerged, they must mark a Stress.

    What corp signature, chemical sheen, or impossible color reveals where this runoff came from? What has learned to thrive here that should not exist?

    Ghost in the Pipes¶

    Residual Resonance carries voices, radio fragments, and memory echoes through the sewer network. A PC who investigates the soundscape can make an Instinct Roll.

    • Critical Success: Learn a valuable clue and a safe or hidden route.
    • Success with Hope: Learn a useful clue, shortcut, or warning.
    • Success with Fear: Learn something true but alarming or incomplete.
    • Any Failure: The echoes become disorienting; mark a Stress to gain one relevant clue.

    Whose voice should not still be here? What secret does the sewer remember better than the people above it?

    • Ghost in the Pipes (Passive) — ** The echoes become disorienting; mark a Stress to gain one relevant clue.

    Whose voice should not still be here? What secret does the sewer remember better than the people above it?

    Shifting Floodgates¶

    Ancient automated gates suddenly open or slam shut, changing the layout of the environment. Separate the party, block retreat, reveal a shortcut, or flush a hazard or adversary into the scene.

    Which route closes at the worst possible time? What forgotten accessway opens that no one expected to still function?

    Biolume Bloom¶

    Mutated fungal mats burst into green-blue light, coating the chamber in sickly illumination. Hidden creatures are revealed and stealth becomes significantly harder until the light fades.

    What shapes or symbols appear in the bloom that suggest Resonance corruption? What living thing is suddenly visible that the PCs wish had stayed hidden?

    Backflow Surge¶

    When a PC rolls with Fear, blackwater surges through the chamber. All creatures in exposed areas must make an Agility Reaction Roll.

    • Targets who fail are knocked down and swept to a nearby zone or hazardous position.
    • Targets who succeed keep their footing but must mark a Stress.

    What debris or evidence comes rushing past in the surge? What new danger does the flood leave behind when it recedes?

    Midnight Streets¶

    The streets at 2 a.m. pulse with the faint afterglow of a city that never quite sleeps but has slumped into a dangerous lull. Neon signage flickers above empty walkways, casting jittery light across rain-sheened pavement. Delivery drones buzz through narrow flight lanes overhead. The transit grid runs on skeleton power, so half the intersections operate on fallback patterns, leaving certain lanes plunged in darkness. Steam rises from cracked vents. The few citizens still awake move fast and keep their heads down.

    Police in Pursuit¶

    Spend 2 Fear to add a police cruiser to the chase in pursuit of the chase.

    Their Chase die starts at the highest value and they can be spotlighted like normal. If they get within range, they will warn all participants in the chase to stop.

    Start a Progress Countdown (4). It ticks down whenever a party member takes an action.

    If this Progress Countdown reaches 0, add another Police cruiser to the chase and the police begin to open fire.

    Attack: +1 | Heavy Pistol | Close | 1d6+2

    • Police in pursuit (Action) — +1 | Heavy Pistol | Close | 1d6+2

    Pedestrians!¶

    Undergrid vagrants walk out into the street. Spend 1 Fear to force a Driving (13) check on one of the players.

    Critical Success: Tick down 1

    Success with Hope: No Advancement

    Success with Fear: Tick up 1

    Failure with Hope: Tick up 2

    Failure with Fear: Tick up 3

    Critical Fail: You clip the vagrant, they are clearly injured by the impact. What do you do?

    • Pedestrians in the way! (Action) — You clip the vagrant, they are clearly injured by the impact. What do you do?

    Slick road¶

    Recent rainfall and leaking hydraulics create slippery patches that can throw off footing, traction, or drone stability.

    Undergrid vagrants walk out into the street. Spend 1 Fear to force a Driving (12) check on one of the players.

    Critical Success: Tick down 2

    Success with Hope: Tick down 1

    Success with Fear: No Advancement

    Failure with Hope: Tick up 1

    Failure with Fear: Tick up 2

    Critical Fail: You spin out Tick up 3

    • Slick road (Action) — You spin out Tick up 3

    Short cut¶

    Any player character who is Driving can Spend 2 Hope to attempt a short cut through the alley. Make a Driving (15) check:

    Critical Success: Chase ends immediately successfully for the players

    Success with Hope: Tick up 3

    Success with Fear: Tick up 2

    Failure with Hope: Tick up 1

    Failure with Fear: No advancement

    Critical Fail: You clip the wall of the short cut and are blocked. Chase ends immediately and unsuccesfully for the players

    • Cut-off (Action) — You clip the wall of the short cut and are blocked. Chase ends immediately and unsuccesfully for the players

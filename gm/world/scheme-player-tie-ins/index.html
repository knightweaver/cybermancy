    Scheme and player tie-in planning — GM¶

    Recorded October 6, 2026. This page preserves the GM's candidate list and its approximate priority. All 13 distinct schemes in the list are queued for player introduction. Each has two available, unrevealed rumors in the database. Queued status does not imply actual PC exposure; rumor order and delivery remain GM choices. Scheme objectives, missions, rumors, and awareness remain controlled by the scheme database; this planning page does not change exposure flags.

    Confirmed decisions¶

    • Stella Brownlock, Xibo, Selka Rivineuve, and Plexitron (Plex) remain active PCs. Prior adventure transcripts and retellings may inform future tie-ins.
    • O2, The Definitive Record, is located in Bombshell.
    • VB2, Green Recovery, moves to Old City. Clearance or redevelopment could threaten Xibo's Shelter; destruction is a possible outcome, not an established event.
    • Dr. Elias Voss is Selka's researcher contact and the same NPC involved in Adventure 3. Elian Voss in the older character notes is a superseded name, not a second person. Existing repository adventure records already use Elias.
    • Little is established about Peggy's current whereabouts. Preserve that uncertainty.
    • Players reveal personal history and affiliations at their own pace. Missions may create opportunities for voluntary disclosure; progress must not require revealing Mithrandir, Astraea, the Adyton, Peggy, or other private backstory. Offer workable ways to contribute through publicly available skills, contacts, or evidence.

    Adventure 3 Session 1 continuity¶

    Source reviewed: Cybermancy - Adventure 3 - Session 1 - full transcript - 09242026.md. This records played events, not the unplayed outcomes of the adventure module. No separate Session 1 retelling was located during this review.

    • Selka describes Voss as scientifically curious but dispassionate, with insufficient attention to the cultural and personal consequences of his research. This is Selka's assessment, not proof of malicious intent.
    • Selka's mother says Voss seemed sincerely curious and concerned, but did not explain more about why. Preserve the difference between these perspectives.
    • Voss became interested in abnormal valley wildlife after hearing about it, was shown an altered raven, and subsequently went farther up the valley.
    • Xibo's investigation found that Voss works through his own corporation and has received angel funding. Xibo obtained a shell-company name and account number as leads for a longer investigation. The transcript does not identify the backer or supply literal names or account details to reproduce here.
    • Selka's inherited condition and the abnormal raven are not established as the same phenomenon or as products of a corporate experiment.

    Next development choices¶

    D2 and O2 provide immediate personal stakes for Stella and Plex. O1 provides an accessible community hook for Xibo without resolving his private history. CG2 can follow Adventure 3 through the funding lead already discovered in play. If the GM chooses it, an indirect ChimeraGene backer can become a future revelation rather than a retroactive claim that the party already established its involvement.

    Introduce rumors before missions where useful. Rumor records include player-facing wording, suggested delivery, and GM context distinguishing established scheme premises from allegations and proposed anecdotes. All scheme and rumor revealed flags remain false, and player-knowledge notes remain empty. Record actual exposure only when it occurs; delivering one rumor does not disclose a scheme’s full objective or hidden sponsor.

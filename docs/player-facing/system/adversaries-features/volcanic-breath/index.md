# Volcanic Breath
<img src="../../../assets/icons/adversaries-features/volcanic-breath.webp" alt="Volcanic Breath" class="item-image" style="width:300px; height:auto;">
## Description
*<p>When the Molten Scourge takes Major damage, roll a <strong>d10</strong>. On a result of 8 or higher, the Molten Scourge breathes a flow of lava in front of them within Far range. All targets in that area must make an Agility Reaction Roll. Targets who fail take <strong>2d10+4</strong> physical damage, mark <strong>1d4</strong> Stress, and are Vulnerable until they clear a Stress. Targets who succeed take half damage and must mark a Stress.</p><p>@Template[type:inFront|range:f]</p>*

# Your Life Is Mine
<img src="../../../assets/icons/adversaries-features/your-life-is-mine.webp" alt="Your Life Is Mine" class="item-image" style="width:300px; height:auto;">
## Description
*<p>Countdown (Loop 2d6). When the Necromancer has marked 6 or more of their HP, activate the countdown. When it triggers, deal <strong>2d10+6</strong> direct magic damage to a target within Close range. The Necromancer then <strong>clears a number of Stress or HP</strong> equal to the number of HP marked by the target from this attack.</p>*

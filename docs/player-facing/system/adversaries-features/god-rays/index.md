## Description
*<p><strong>Mark a Stress</strong> to reflect a sliver of divinity as a searing beam of light that hits up to twenty targets within Very Far range. Targets must make a Presence Reaction Roll, with disadvantage if they are marked Guilty. Targets who fail take <strong>4d6+12</strong> magic damage. Targets who succeed take half damage.</p>*

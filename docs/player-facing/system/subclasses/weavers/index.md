## Features
- <a href='../../features/coding-reality/'><img src='../../../assets/icons/features/coding-reality.webp' alt='Coding Reality' class='item-image' style='width:120px; height:auto;'></a><br> <a href='../../features/coding-reality/'><strong>Coding Reality</strong></a> — _Foundation_: <p>You hotfix the scene with a split-second patch. </p><p>Once per scene, you may invoke a flashback to declare that you previously acquired, prepared, or cached a small magical or technological item you now need (the GM may forbid items that would trivialize the scene; no ammunition, explosives, or excavation tools).  </p><p>Flashback cost may be reduced due to the ephemeral nature of the item, and may use hope and/or stress resources.  The item persists for 5 seconds (long enough for one immediate use) before the weight of collective perception forces it to derez.</p>
- <a href='../../features/neuromancy-protocol/'><img src='../../../assets/icons/features/neuromancy-protocol.webp' alt='Neuromancy Protocol' class='item-image' style='width:120px; height:auto;'></a><br> <a href='../../features/neuromancy-protocol/'><strong>Neuromancy Protocol</strong></a> — _Specialization_: <p>Spellwork and software share the same bus. </p><p>After you succeed on a Spellcast roll using an Arcana card, you may immediately take an additional action to perform a Circuit-domain hack or Circuit feature you have access to. </p><p>This follow-up action does not cost Hope (if it normally would), but it can still have other costs (like marking Stress) and it must target the same system, scene, or opposition when reasonable.</p>
- <a href='../../features/quantum-casting/'><img src='../../../assets/icons/features/quantum-casting.webp' alt='Quantum Casting' class='item-image' style='width:120px; height:auto;'></a><br> <a href='../../features/quantum-casting/'><strong>Quantum Casting</strong></a> — _Mastery_: <p>Your technical expertise amplifies your spellwork. </p><p>When you spend Hope to apply an Experience bonus to an Arcana Spellcast roll, and that Experience is clearly related to technology, hacking, or digital systems, double the Experience’s modifier for that roll (for example, +2 becomes +4).</p>

# Coding Reality
<img src="../../../assets/icons/features/coding-reality.webp" alt="Coding Reality" class="item-image" style="width:300px; height:auto;">
## Description
*<p>You hotfix the scene with a split-second patch. </p><p>Once per scene, you may invoke a flashback to declare that you previously acquired, prepared, or cached a small magical or technological item you now need (the GM may forbid items that would trivialize the scene; no ammunition, explosives, or excavation tools).  </p><p>Flashback cost may be reduced due to the ephemeral nature of the item, and may use hope and/or stress resources.  The item persists for 5 seconds (long enough for one immediate use) before the weight of collective perception forces it to derez.</p>*

## Description
*<p><em>It speaks your syntax and calls you by your old handle.</em><br /><br /></p><p class="Card-Feature">Spawns a self-aware daemon that hounds and taunts across systems until removed.</p><p></p><p>The ICE attempts to install a daemon on your computer. ## Actions
- <div markdown='1'>**Daemon Host**<br>*It speaks your syntax and calls you by your old handle.

- <div markdown='1'>**Daemon Host**<br>*It speaks your syntax and calls you by your old handle.

Spawns a self-aware daemon that hounds and taunts across systems until removed.

The ICE attempts to install a daemon on your computer.  As a Reaction, make a Spellcasting (Hacking) roll against the Difficulty of this ICE to prevent this installation.

If the daemon is successfully installed, all subsequent Spellcasting (Hacking) checks are at -2.  To remove the daemon, requires a Short Rest downtime activity.

While running the daemon provides running commentary on the deficiencies of your coding, hair cut, and life choices in the voice of an Italian grandmother.*</div>

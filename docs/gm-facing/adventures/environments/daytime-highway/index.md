## Features
### Slick road

Recent rainfall and leaking hydraulics create slippery patches that can throw off footing, traction, or drone stability.

Undergrid vagrants walk out into the street. Spend 1 Fear to force a Driving (12) check on one of the players.

Critical Success: Tick down 2

Success with Hope: Tick down 1

Success with Fear: No Advancement

Failure with Hope: Tick up 1

Failure with Fear: Tick up 2

Critical Fail: You spin out Tick up 3

- **Slick road** (*Action*) —  You spin out Tick up 3

### Cut-off

The party may Spend 1 Hope each or the GM may Spend 2 Fear to trigger a constriction in the traffic against the opponent.

Depending no which side triggers this effect, the other side must as a Reaction make an immediate Driving check for the Difficulty of the environment.

Critical Success: No Advancement

Success: Tick up 1

Failure: Tick up 2

Critical Fail: You clip one of the cars in traffic, Tick up 3 and they got your license plate number

- **Cut-off** (*Action*) —  You clip one of the cars in traffic, Tick up 3 and they got your license plate number

### Police in Pursuit

Spend 2 Fear to add a police cruiser to the chase in pursuit of the chase.

Their Chase die starts at the highest value and they can be spotlighted like normal. If they get within range, they will warn all participants in the chase to stop.

Start a Progress Countdown (4). It ticks down whenever a party member takes an action.

If this Progress Countdown reaches 0, add another Police cruiser to the chase and the police begin to open fire. 

Attack: +1 | Heavy Pistol | Close | 1d6+2

- **Police in pursuit** (*Action*) —  +1 | Heavy Pistol | Close | 1d6+2

## Notes
Environment: Rush-Hour Elevated Highway

A dense flow of commuters fills the multilane highway. Autonomous cars hold tight formation while human drivers weave unpredictably. Traffic speed oscillates between smooth motion and abrupt gridlock as routing algorithms struggle with peak load. Police speed traps sit on overpasses and median cutouts. Farther ahead, robotic construction rigs crawl along active repair zones, narrowing lanes and throwing sensor noise into the air.

Complicating Features

Sudden Traffic Compression
Routing shifts or lane merges can collapse flow instantly, forcing hard braking or rapid lane changes.

Police Speed Traps
High-resolution lidar and drone patrols sweep for velocity spikes and unusual maneuvering.

Human Driver Variability
Commuters make late merges, emergency stops, or erratic lane shifts that disrupt predictable movement.

Autonomous Platoons
Tightly grouped auto-cars leave little physical space; breaking into a platoon requires precision or force.

Construction Rigs
Large robotic equipment drifts across marked boundaries, shedding debris and throwing off targeting or sensor locks.

Sensor Interference
High signal density from vehicles, signage, and enforcement systems can degrade comms or targeting feeds.

This setup supports a high-velocity chase with constant positional constraints and external pressure from civilian and enforcement systems.

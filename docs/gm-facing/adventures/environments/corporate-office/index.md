## Features
### Talkative Intern

You are accosted by a talkative intern that is exceptionally interested in your background, the project you are working on an just about anything that might help advance his career at the company.

Start a Countdown clock (3).  When the clock reaches 0 you have successfully ditched the intern.  You can advance the clock by :

Making a successful check for an appropriate social skill

Mark 1 Stress

While the intern is with you all actions not attempting to address the intern are at disadvantage.

### Daemon Host

It speaks your syntax and calls you by your old handle.

Spawns a self-aware daemon that hounds and taunts across systems until removed.

The ICE attempts to install a daemon on your computer.  As a Reaction, make a Spellcasting (Hacking) roll against the Difficulty of this ICE to prevent this installation.

If the daemon is successfully installed, all subsequent Spellcasting (Hacking) checks are at -2.  To remove the daemon, requires a Short Rest downtime activity.

While running the daemon provides running commentary on the deficiencies of your coding, hair cut, and life choices in the voice of an Italian grandmother.

- **Daemon Host** (*Action*) — It speaks your syntax and calls you by your old handle.

Spawns a self-aware daemon that hunts you across systems until destroyed or appeased.

### Siren

Your comm buzzes with your own voice: ‘Come home.’

Generates perfect voices and prompts to lure you deeper into the system.

- **Siren** (*Action*) —  ‘Come home.’

Generates perfect voices and prompts to lure you deeper into the system.

### Security patrol

The office manager is walking through your area with a Head Guard and 2 Corporate Guards.

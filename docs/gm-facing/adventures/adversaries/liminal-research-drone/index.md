## Features
### Many Hands

When two or more Drones engage the same target, their attacks gain advantage.

### Restraint Protocol

Make an attack against a target within Very Close range. On a success, the target must mark a Stress or become Restrained until they next act.

### Wall Transit

Mark a Stress to move through vents, ceiling rails, maintenance ducts, or narrow openings up to Far range.

### Shared Signal

When one Drone is defeated, another Drone within Close range immediately moves and takes the spotlight.

### Task Continuity

Unless directly threatened, the Drones continue lab tasks during combat—cleaning blood, adjusting sensors, and logging data.

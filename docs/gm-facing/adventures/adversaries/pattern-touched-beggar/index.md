## Motives & Tactics
confuse, disorient, convert, suborn

## Features
### Enervating Blast

Spend a Fear to make a standard attack against a target within range. On a success, the target must mark a Stress.

### Slippery

When the Sorcerer takes damage from an attack, they can teleport up to Far range.

### Arcane Artillery

Spend a Fear to unleash a precise hail of magical blasts. All targets in the scene must make an Agility Reaction Roll. Targets who fail take 2d6 magic damage. Targets who succeed take half damage.

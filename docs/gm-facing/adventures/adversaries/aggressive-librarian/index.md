# Aggressive Librarian
<img src="../../../assets/images/adversaries/aggressive-librarian.png" alt="Aggressive Librarian" class="item-image" style="width:300px; height:auto;">

<div class="item-flavor" markdown="1">
A stern Seattle librarian and expert local historian whose Cybermancy summons spectral projections shaped by the books around her.

FAST PLAYOpening: Be stern but helpful. Answer sincere questions and use Reference Authority during research.

Default: Correct bad behavior first. Use Withering Correction against bullying or deception.

If Disrespected: Resolve Respect the Collection. Remain Hostile until the offender makes amends or leaves.

If Hostile: Use Spectral Citation normally. Spend Fear on The Stacks Answer Back against clustered threats.

Remember: Every magical attack reflects nearby books; only its appearance changes.

Goal: Be an intimidating guardian and expert first, dangerous Cybermancer second.

## Experiences
- **Seattle History +3**

## Motives & Tactics
answer sincere questions, protect the collection, enforce proper handling, correct misinformation, educate before punishing, evoke stories from the stacks

## Features
### Reference Authority

While cooperative, she truthfully answers questions about Seattle, the Resonance, or library-held information. A PC researching with her gains advantage on their first relevant action roll.

### Withering Correction

When a PC rolls 16 or lower to deceive, intimidate, or browbeat her, they mark 1 Stress.

### Respect the Collection

When someone damages, defaces, steals, or repeatedly mishandles library materials - or threatens her - they make a Presence Reaction Roll (15). On a failure, mark 2 Stress. She becomes Hostile until they make meaningful amends or leave.

### The Stacks Answer Back

While Hostile, spend a Fear to summon spectral figures within Far range. Targets within Very Close make an Agility Reaction Roll (14), taking 2d6+3 magical damage on a failure or half on a success. Their appearance reflects nearby books.

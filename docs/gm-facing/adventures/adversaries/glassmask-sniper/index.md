## Features
### Assassin Poisoner

The Lookout has advantage on attacks if they are Hidden.

### Careful Aim

If the Glassmask Sniper doesn't have a token on it, place a token on their stat block.  When you spotlight the Glassmask Sniper and they have a token, clear the token and they can attack with advantage and do 2d8+2 damage.

- **Attack** (*Action*) — Make an attack against a Vulnerable target within Far range. On a success, mark a Stress to deal 3d4+8 physical damage.

# Cavelor Finn


<div class="item-flavor" markdown="1">
A lean half-elf assassin who treats observation as the real job and violence as an optional conclusion. Finn works from prepared hides with suppressed weapons, custom toxins, and multiple escape routes, refusing any fight that becomes fair.

FAST PLAYOpening: Shadow the team unseen until Finn has a clean assassination opportunity or another compelling reason to reveal himself.

Default: From Hidden, use Precision Rifle to trigger Prepared Shot against exposed prey, then reset with Ghost Position rather than trade attacks.

If Pressed: Use Exit Strategy when disengagement matters. Use Dead Drop when preparation can restore concealment, create escape, or disrupt pursuit.

Fallback: Use Suppressed Pistol when close engagement is already advantageous; if the fight becomes fair, break contact.

Remember: Relentless (2) permits a second spotlight but does not make it free. Any hit that marks HP triggers Finn's Compound.

Goal: Make the PCs feel watched, suddenly endangered, then uncertain whether Finn is truly gone.
</div>

## Features
### Relentless (2)

Cavelor Finn can be spotlighted up to two times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight him.

### Precision Rifle

Make an attack against a target within Very Far range with a +4 attack bonus. On a success, deal 2d10+4 physical damage.

### Ghost Position

Mark a Stress to become Hidden and immediately move anywhere within Close range that provides plausible concealment or cover.

### Prepared Shot

When Finn makes a successful Precision Rifle attack while Hidden, deal 3d10+4 physical damage instead of the standard Precision Rifle damage.

### Finn's Compound

A target who marks HP from one of Finn's attacks becomes Vulnerable until they clear an HP.

### Exit Strategy

When Finn marks 2 or more HP from a single attack, or when a PC moves into Melee range of him, he can mark a Stress to immediately move anywhere within Far range that he could plausibly reach using terrain, a prepared line, concealed passage, vehicle, grapnel, or similar escape route. He becomes Hidden at the end of this movement if he reaches cover.

### Dead Drop

Spend a Fear to reveal that Finn prepared a surveillance device, concealed weapon cache, escape line, or remotely triggered distraction at a plausible location in the scene. Finn may immediately use that preparation to either move anywhere within Far range and become Hidden, or force all creatures within Very Close range of the revealed device to make an Agility Reaction Roll (16). On a failure, they mark 2 Stress and become Vulnerable until their next action.

# Clevermask
<img src="../../../assets/images/adversaries/clevermask.png" alt="Clevermask" class="item-image" style="width:300px; height:auto;">

<div class="item-flavor" markdown="1">
An ordinary-looking Cascade raccoon whose bright eyes and precise forepaw gestures reveal startling intelligence. It is curious rather than hostile and flees from violence.

FAST PLAYOpening: Observe the PCs curiously and try to communicate. Investigate unfamiliar technology or obvious Cybermancy.

Default: Use Hands That Speak to exchange simple ideas. Gentle treatment plus food can make the Clevermask Friendly automatically.

If Friendly: Answer what it could reasonably know and guide the PCs through familiar terrain or toward an observable clue.

If Threatened: Trigger Nope and escape if possible. Use Defensive Scratch only if cornered with no plausible escape.

Remember: After a PC successfully communicates once, that PC has advantage on later attempts to understand its gestures this scene.

Goal: Reward patience, curiosity, and kindness with information and exploration help; violence should normally end the encounter.

## Motives & Tactics
investigate strangers, communicate, barter for food, learn, avoid violence, guide trusted creatures

## Features
### Hands That Speak

The Clevermask communicates through an improvised language of hand signs, posture, pointing, facial expression, and objects. A creature that spends a short time attempting to communicate with it can make an Instinct or Presence Roll (12). On a success, exchange one reasonably simple idea or question. On a success with Hope, exchange two ideas or communicate one moderately complicated concept. On a failure, the meaning remains unclear, but the Clevermask does not become hostile. Once a creature has successfully communicated with the Clevermask, they have advantage on subsequent rolls to understand its gestures during the scene.

### Food Makes Friends

If a creature treats the Clevermask gently and offers it food, it becomes Friendly toward that creature without requiring a roll. While Friendly, the Clevermask will answer simple questions as best it can, warn the party about nearby dangers it knows about, retrieve or point out small objects, follow the group for a reasonable distance, and act as a guide through territory it knows. Attacking it immediately ends this benefit.

### Bright-Eyed Guide

While the party willingly follows a Friendly Clevermask through terrain it knows, the first PC to make a roll to navigate, track, locate a route, or avoid a natural hazard has advantage. Additionally, once per scene, the Clevermask can point out something the PCs might otherwise miss: a hidden trail, tracks, a safe crossing, a concealed entrance, an approaching predator, an object, or a recent disturbance. The GM should only reveal something the Clevermask could plausibly have observed.

- **Bright-Eyed Guide** (*Passive*) —  a hidden trail, tracks, a safe crossing, a concealed entrance, an approaching predator, an object, or a recent disturbance. The GM should only reveal something the Clevermask could plausibly have observed.

### Oh! What's That?

When a PC displays an unfamiliar object, activates unusual technology, performs obvious Cybermancy, or otherwise does something the Clevermask has never seen before, it may cautiously approach to investigate. A PC who allows it to examine the object can immediately attempt Hands That Speak with advantage.

### Nope

When the Clevermask is targeted by an attack, takes damage, or reasonably believes a creature intends to capture it, it immediately moves anywhere within Far range toward cover or an escape route and becomes Hidden if possible. If there is a plausible route out of the scene, it escapes. The Clevermask does not return during that scene unless the GM has a compelling fictional reason.

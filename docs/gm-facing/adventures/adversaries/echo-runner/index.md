## Features
### Slippery

When the Runner takes damage from an attack, they can teleport up to Far range.

### Chaotic Flux

Make an attack against up to three targets within Very Close range. Mark a Stress to deal 2d6+3 magic damage to targets the Hexer succeeded against.

- **Attack** (*Action*) — Make an attack against up to two targets within Very Close range. Mark a Stress to deal 1d6+3 magic damage to targets the Hexer succeeded against.

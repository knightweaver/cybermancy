# Abraxas modified Deer


<div class="item-flavor" markdown="1">
A once-natural deer twisted by Abraxan ritual bleed, with blackened branching antlers, ember-lit eyes, and a violent compulsion to charge anything that shows fear.

FAST PLAYOpening: Use Antler Rush from Close against an exposed target so the Deer arrives violently and establishes its charge-and-pursue behavior.

Default: Attack with Antlers in Melee. If distance opens again, look for another useful line for Antler Rush.

On PC Fear: Use Ritual Frenzy when a PC within Close rolls with Fear, especially if it lets the Deer punish a target who thought they were safe.

Pressure: Keep pursuing exposed or fearful prey rather than holding ground like a trained defender.

Goal: Show ritual corruption through relentless animal aggression: charge, scatter the group, and make Fear attract immediate danger.

## Experiences

## Motives & Tactics
charge exposed targets, gore, pursue fear, defend the ritual ground

## Features
### Antler Rush

Make an attack against a target within Close range. On a success, mark a Stress to move into Melee range of the target, deal 1d8+3 physical damage, and knock them back to Very Close range.

### Ritual Frenzy

When a PC within Close range rolls with Fear, you can mark a Stress to move the Deer into Melee range of that PC and make a standard attack against them.

## Features
### Rally Guards

Spend 2 Fear to spotlight the Head Guard and up to 2d4 allies within Far range.

@Template[type:emanation|range:f]

- **Rally** (*Action*) — f]

### On My Signal

Countdown (5). When the Head Guard is in the spotlight for the first time, activate the countdown. It ticks down when a PC makes an attack roll. When it triggers, all Corporate Guards within Far range make a standard attack with advantage against the nearest target within their range. If any attacks succeed on the same target, combine their damage.

@Template[type:emanation|range:f]

- **Start Countdown** (*Reaction*) — Countdown (5). When the Head Guard is in the spotlight for the first time, activate the countdown. It ticks down when a PC makes an attack roll. When it triggers, all Archer Guards within Far range make a standard attack with advantage against the nearest target within their range. ### Momentum

When the Head Guard makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.

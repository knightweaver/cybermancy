# Preferential Treatment
<img src="../../../assets/icons/adversaries-features/preferential-treatment.webp" alt="Preferential Treatment" class="item-image" style="width:300px; height:auto;">
## Description
*<p>A PC who succeeds on a Presence Roll against the Merchant gains a discount on purchases. A PC who fails on a Presence Roll against the Merchant must pay more and has disadvantage on future Presence Rolls against the Merchant.</p>*

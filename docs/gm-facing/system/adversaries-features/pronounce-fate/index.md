# Pronounce Fate

## Description
*<p><strong>Spend a Fear</strong> to present a target within Far range with a vision of their personal nightmare. The target must make a Knowledge Reaction Roll. On a failure, they lose all Hope and take <strong>2d20+4</strong> direct magic damage. On a success, they take half damage and lose a Hope.</p>*

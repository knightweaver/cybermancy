# Charging Bull
<img src="../../../assets/icons/adversaries-features/charging-bull.webp" alt="Charging Bull" class="item-image" style="width:300px; height:auto;">
## Description
*<p><strong>Mark a Stress</strong> to charge through a group within Close range and make an attack against all targets in the Minotaur’s path. Targets the Minotaur succeeds against take <strong>2d6+8</strong> physical damage and are knocked back to Very Far range. If a target is knocked into a solid object or another creature, they take an extra <strong>1d6</strong> damage (combine the damage).</p>*
